Who We Are

Built on Decades of Trust & Purpose

GWEN Consulting (Global Wellness Education Network Limited) is a UK-registered consultancy founded on a friendship forged in the 1980s and a shared belief that wellness is a universal right, not a luxury.

We sit at the precise intersection that no other consultancy occupies — deep African cultural fluency, clinically-validated wellness methodology, and the corporate governance required for high-value international contracts.
